Privacy Policy

This is a personal blog, published as a static website. It is designed to be simple and privacy-friendly: there is nothing here that is trying to track you.

Last updated: 7 October 2025

What this site does not do

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics, tracking scripts, pixels or fingerprinting are used.
  • No personal data is collected, stored, sold or shared by me.
  • No accounts, comments, newsletters or forms exist on this site.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub automatically records standard server log data when a page is requested. This may include your IP address, browser type, referring page, and the date and time of the request.

This data is processed by GitHub for operating and securing the service, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. I do not have access to these logs beyond what GitHub publicly provides, and I do not use them to identify visitors.

Third-party content

Pages on this site may link to external websites. If embedded third-party content is ever added (for example videos or comment widgets), this policy will be updated to reflect it. External sites linked from here have their own privacy policies, which I do not control.

Your rights

Under regulations such as the EU GDPR, you have rights regarding personal data held about you. Since this site does not collect personal data, there is generally nothing to access or delete. If you have questions or concerns, you can contact me through GitHub.

Changes to this policy

If this site changes in ways that affect privacy (for example, adding analytics or embedded content), this page will be updated accordingly. The date at the top reflects the last revision.
